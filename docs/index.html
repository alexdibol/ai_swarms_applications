The pedagogical objective

Teach AI as
institutional design.

The notebooks are constructed as inspectable laboratories for understanding how multi-agent systems should be designed when finance contains competing forms of expertise, uncertainty, hard constraints, and consequences.

The objective is not to celebrate swarm intelligence as a black box. It is to make the architecture visible: what each agent sees, how disagreement survives, which layers are deterministic, what evidence can overrule the generative system, and where human authority remains accountable.

01

Substantive heterogeneity

Different agents represent distinct failure surfaces, professional mandates, investment philosophies, or scientific traditions.

02

Preserve disagreement

Dispersion, dissent, and minority reports are retained as information rather than erased by premature consensus.

03

Separate powers

Generative systems search and interpret; deterministic systems calculate, estimate, optimize, and enforce hard constraints.

04

External truth surfaces

Pricing engines, holdout data, current law, portfolio constraints, realized outcomes, and human authority remain outside the swarm.

05

Provenance

The system should retain who proposed what, on which evidence, under which mandate, with which objections and results.

06

Governed adaptation

As autonomy rises, constitutional limits, auditability, reversibility, and human oversight become more important.

The discipline that survives every episode

More intelligence.
More explicit
governance.

Generative intelligence expands the hypothesis space; deterministic systems enforce what is already known; external evidence adjudicates uncertainty; accountable governance controls consequential action.
Bounded mandatesAgents receive explicit roles, permissions, evidence contracts, and responsibilities.
Hard constraintsPortfolio limits, theory, law, prudential rules, and institutional boundaries are not reduced to soft preferences.
Independent adjudicationNumerical engines, holdout samples, realized outcomes, and current authoritative facts sit outside the generative layer.
Provenance and reversibilityDecisions, dissent, memory, reputation, and organizational changes remain reconstructable and governable.
